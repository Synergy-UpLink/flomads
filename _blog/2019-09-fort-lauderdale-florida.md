Fort Lauderdale is a city on Florida's southeastern coast and the county seat of Broward County, between Miami and Palm Beach. It has around 180,000 residents within the city limits.

## A bit of history

The city is named for a series of forts built during the Second Seminole War in 1838, one of which honored Major William Lauderdale. It was incorporated in 1911. In 1960 the film *Where the Boys Are* helped turn it into the best-known spring-break destination in the country, a reputation the city has since worked hard to move beyond.

## What it's known for

- **Waterways.** A network of canals, bays, and the New River gives it the nickname "Venice of America."
- **Yachting.** It is often called the yachting capital of the world, and its International Boat Show is among the largest anywhere.
- **Port Everglades.** One of the busiest cruise ports in the world and a major departure point for the Caribbean and the Bahamas.
- **Las Olas Boulevard.** The city's main shopping and dining street.
- **Beaches.** A long, wide stretch of Atlantic beach.

## Climate

Tropical, with a hot, wet summer season and warm, dry winters.
