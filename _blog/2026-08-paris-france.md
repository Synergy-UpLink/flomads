Paris is the capital of France, in the north of the country on the Seine. About 2 million people live within the city limits and around 12 million in the greater Paris region, which makes it one of the largest urban areas in Europe.

## A bit of history

The city began as a settlement of the Parisii, a Gallic tribe, on the Île de la Cité in the 3rd century BC. The Romans called it Lutetia. Notre-Dame cathedral was begun in 1163 on that same island. After the 2019 fire it was restored and reopened to the public in late 2024. In the 1850s and '60s, Baron Haussmann rebuilt large parts of the city, which is why so many streets share the same cream-colored stone, wrought-iron balconies, and zinc roofs.

## What it's known for

- **The Eiffel Tower.** Completed in 1889 for the World's Fair and about 330 meters tall.
- **The Louvre.** Regularly cited as the most-visited art museum in the world.
- **Arrondissements.** Twenty districts that spiral outward from the center, like a snail shell.
- **The Métro.** Opened in 1900 and still the fastest way across town.
- **Cafés, bakeries, and markets.** The daily rhythm of the city.

## Climate

Temperate and often gray, with mild summers and cool, damp winters.
