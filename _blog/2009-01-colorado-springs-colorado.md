Colorado Springs is the county seat of El Paso County, Colorado, at the eastern foot of the Rocky Mountains about 60 miles south of Denver. It sits at roughly 6,000 feet above sea level, and with about 480,000 residents it is the second most populous city in the state.

## A bit of history

The city was founded in 1871 by General William Jackson Palmer, a railroad builder who intended it as a resort town. The nearby mineral springs and mountain scenery helped it grow into one. The peak that dominates the skyline is named for Zebulon Pike, the explorer who sighted it in 1806 but never reached the summit.

## What it's known for

- **Pikes Peak.** At 14,115 feet, one of Colorado's famous fourteeners, reachable by road, cog railway, or on foot.
- **Garden of the Gods.** A public park of towering red sandstone formations, designated a National Natural Landmark.
- **The military.** The U.S. Air Force Academy, founded in 1954, is just north of the city, and NORAD's Cheyenne Mountain complex is on its southwest edge.
- **Olympic and Paralympic training.** The U.S. Olympic & Paralympic Training Center is based here.

## Climate

Sunny and dry, with four seasons and snow that rarely stays long on the ground.
