South Benfleet is a town in the Castle Point district of Essex, about 30 miles east of London, on the edge of the Thames Estuary marshes. Benfleet Creek separates it from Canvey Island.

## A bit of history

In 894 the area was the site of the Battle of Benfleet, when a Danish Viking force camped here was defeated by English forces that included Edward the Elder, son of King Alfred. Local tradition holds that a church was founded in thanksgiving, and the Church of St Mary the Virgin, now a Grade I listed building, stands on that site today. (Some sources date the battle to 893.)

## Around the area

- **Hadleigh Castle.** The ruins of a 13th-century castle overlook the estuary a few miles away.
- **Canvey Island.** Just across the creek, with its own sea-wall and flood-defense history.
- **A small curiosity.** Wikipedia notes the junction by St Mary's and The Anchor pub as the site of the UK's first "overrunable" mini roundabout.

## Getting around

Benfleet station is on the c2c line to London Fenchurch Street and Southend, so the capital is an easy train ride away.
