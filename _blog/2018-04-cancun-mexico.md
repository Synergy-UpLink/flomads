Cancún is a city in the Mexican state of Quintana Roo, on the northeastern tip of the Yucatán Peninsula, fronting the Caribbean Sea. It has close to 900,000 residents.

## A bit of history

Cancún is unusual because it was planned. In the early 1970s the Mexican government picked this quiet stretch of coast, then a small fishing area, and developed it into a resort destination. It has grown very fast since. The name is commonly said to come from Mayan words for "nest of snakes."

## What it's known for

- **The Hotel Zone.** A long, narrow strip of land between the Caribbean and a lagoon, about 22 kilometers long and shaped roughly like the number seven.
- **Beaches.** White sand and turquoise water.
- **Maya heritage.** Small ruins sit inside the city, and Chichén Itzá is a few hours' drive inland.
- **Nearby islands.** Isla Mujeres is a short ferry ride away, with Cozumel farther down the coast.

## Climate

Tropical, warm all year, with a rainy season from roughly June to October and a hurricane season that overlaps it.
