Ris-Orangis is a commune in the Essonne department, in the southern suburbs of Paris. It sits on the Seine about 22 kilometers from the center of the capital and has roughly 30,000 residents. People from Ris-Orangis are called *Rissois*.

## A bit of history

The commune was created in 1793 by merging two older villages, Ris and Orangis. Its town hall, in the Ris half, occupies a building that began life around 1700 as the Château de Fromont.

## Getting around

Three stations on Paris's RER D line serve the commune (Ris-Orangis, Grand-Bourg, and Orangis–Bois de l'Épine), which puts central Paris within a short train ride.

## What it's like

Mostly residential, with parks, the river, and the kind of everyday French suburban life that never makes the guidebooks. It is also a good reminder that the Paris region is far larger than the city inside the périphérique.
