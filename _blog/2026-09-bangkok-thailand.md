Bangkok is the capital and largest city of Thailand, on the Chao Phraya River near the Gulf of Thailand. Roughly 10 million people live in the city proper, and the wider metropolitan area is considerably larger.

## What's in a name

Thais call the city *Krung Thep*, "City of Angels." Its full ceremonial name, *Krung Thep Maha Nakhon* and so on for a good while after that, is widely cited as one of the longest place names in the world.

## A bit of history

Bangkok became the capital in 1782, when King Rama I, founder of the Chakri dynasty, moved the royal seat across the river from Thonburi and began building the Grand Palace. The city was once crisscrossed by canals (*khlongs*) and earned the nickname "Venice of the East." Many canals were later filled in to make roads, though some still carry boats. The BTS Skytrain opened in 1999 and the MRT subway in 2004, which changed how the city moves.

## What it's known for

- **Temples.** Wat Pho houses a reclining Buddha around 46 meters long, and Wat Arun, the "Temple of Dawn," stands on the river's west bank.
- **The Grand Palace.** The former royal residence is still used for ceremonies.
- **Markets.** Chatuchak Weekend Market is one of the largest in the world.
- **Food.** Street food is a way of life here, from noodle carts to the Chinatown stalls along Yaowarat Road.

## Climate

Hot and humid all year, with a rainy season that runs roughly from May to October.
