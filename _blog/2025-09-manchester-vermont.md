Manchester is a town in Bennington County, in southwestern Vermont, with the Green Mountains to the east and the Taconic range to the west. It has a little over 4,000 residents and has long been a resort town for hiking, skiing, and fly-fishing.

## A bit of history

Tourists have been coming to Manchester since the 19th century, when grand hotels and the arrival of the railroad made it a summer destination. The Equinox, a historic resort in the center of town, traces its roots back to the 1850s.

## What it's known for

- **Hildene.** The 1905 summer home of Robert Todd Lincoln, Abraham Lincoln's son, now a museum with preserved gardens and trails.
- **Orvis.** The fly-fishing company was founded in Manchester in 1856 and is still headquartered in the area.
- **The Battenkill.** A river with a long reputation among trout anglers.
- **Equinox Mountain.** A prominent peak of the Taconic range that rises above the town.
- **Outdoors.** The Long Trail and the Appalachian Trail run through the nearby Green Mountains.

## Climate

Cold, snowy winters; mild summers; and a very strong fall foliage season.
