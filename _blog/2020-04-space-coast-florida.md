The Space Coast is the stretch of Florida's Atlantic shoreline in Brevard County, roughly 70 miles long, between Titusville in the north and Palm Bay in the south. It gets its nickname from the launch facilities at Cape Canaveral and Kennedy Space Center.

## A bit of history

NASA has launched from this coast since the early 1960s. Kennedy Space Center, on Merritt Island, sent the Apollo 11 crew toward the Moon in July 1969, and the Space Shuttle flew from here from 1981 until its retirement in 2011. Today, commercial launch companies use the same coast, and launches are frequent.

## What it's known for

- **Launches.** Visible from beaches and causeways for miles around.
- **Kennedy Space Center Visitor Complex.** Rockets, a shuttle orbiter, and bus tours of the launch pads.
- **The Indian River Lagoon.** A long, shallow estuary behind the barrier islands, rich in wildlife.
- **Wildlife refuges.** Merritt Island National Wildlife Refuge and Canaveral National Seashore share land with the space program.
- **Beaches and surfing.** Cocoa Beach is the best-known surf town on the coast.

## Climate

Subtropical, with hot, humid summers, mild winters, and a hurricane season from June to November.
