Exuma is a district of the Bahamas made up of more than 365 islands and cays, strung out over about 120 miles of the Atlantic southeast of Nassau. It had around 7,300 residents at the 2022 census, most of them on Great Exuma, the largest island.

## A bit of history

The Lucayan people called the chain *Curateo*, roughly "outer far distant land." George Town, the district's capital on Great Exuma, was founded in 1793 and named for King George III, to whom the settlers remained loyal.

## What it's known for

- **The Exuma Cays Land and Sea Park.** Established in 1958, it was one of the first protected areas of its kind in the world. It covers about 456 square kilometers and is a no-fishing zone.
- **The swimming pigs.** A colony of feral pigs lives on Big Major Cay, uninhabited by people and famous for wading out to meet boats.
- **Thunderball Grotto.** A sea cave you can snorkel into, which appeared in two James Bond films.
- **Clear water.** Shallow sandbars, rock iguanas, and nurse sharks at Compass Cay.

## Getting around

By boat, mostly. The Exumas are a classic cruising ground.
