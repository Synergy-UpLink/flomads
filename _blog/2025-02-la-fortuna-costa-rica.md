La Fortuna is a district in San Carlos canton, Alajuela Province, in the northern lowlands of Costa Rica. It sits at about 250 meters above sea level and had a little over 15,000 residents at the 2011 census.

## Arenal Volcano

The reason most people come is Arenal Volcano, a cone-shaped stratovolcano about 1,650 meters tall that dominates the view from town. Its deadly eruption in 1968 destroyed nearby villages and killed 82 people (La Fortuna itself was spared). Arenal then erupted almost continuously for decades, until the activity stopped around 2010. It is now quiet, and much of the surrounding land is Arenal Volcano National Park.

## What it's known for

- **La Fortuna Waterfall.** Roughly 70 meters tall, reached by a long staircase down into the forest.
- **Hot springs.** Volcanic heat feeds several resorts and a few free spots along the river.
- **Cerro Chato.** A dormant volcano next door with a crater lake.
- **Lake Arenal.** A large reservoir to the west, popular for wind sports and fishing.

## Climate

Warm and humid, with rain through much of the year and a short drier stretch.
