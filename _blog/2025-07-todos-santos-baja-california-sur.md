Todos Santos is a small town on the Pacific coast of the Baja California Sur peninsula in Mexico, about midway between Cabo San Lucas and La Paz. It had roughly 7,000 residents at the 2020 census.

## A bit of history

The town dates to 1723, when the Jesuit missionary Jaime Bravo founded a mission at this oasis. Water from the nearby Sierra de la Laguna keeps the area green in an otherwise dry landscape, and in the 19th century it supported a sugar cane industry with several mills. In 2006 the Mexican government gave it the *Pueblo Mágico* designation, a program that recognizes towns for their historic and cultural character.

## What it's known for

- **Art.** A long-established gallery and artist community in restored adobe buildings.
- **Surf.** Playa Los Cerritos and other Pacific beaches nearby attract surfers.
- **The Hotel California.** A boutique hotel in town is tied by local legend to the Eagles song of the same name.
- **A slower pace.** Quieter than Cabo, with an easy walkability.

## Climate

Desert climate: warm and sunny most of the year, with a short rainy season in late summer and early autumn.
