Albrighton is a large village in Shropshire, England, about 8 miles northwest of Wolverhampton and 11 miles northeast of Bridgnorth. It has a little over 4,000 residents and is the most easterly settlement in the county.

## A bit of history

Albrighton appears in the Domesday Book of 1086 (as *Albricston*) and received a market charter in 1303. The railway came in 1849, and the station is still on the line between Wolverhampton and Shrewsbury, which turned the village into a popular spot for commuters to the West Midlands.

## Nearby

- **Boscobel House.** A few miles away is the house where the future Charles II hid after the Battle of Worcester in 1651, famously spending a day in an oak tree that became known as the Royal Oak.
- **RAF Cosford.** Home to the Royal Air Force Museum Midlands.
- **David Austin Roses.** The renowned English rose breeder is based on the edge of the village.
- **The M54.** Which puts Telford, Wolverhampton, and Birmingham within easy reach.

## What it's like

Quiet, green, and quintessentially English, with village shops, pubs, and a cricket club.
