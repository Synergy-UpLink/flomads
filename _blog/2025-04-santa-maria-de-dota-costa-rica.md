Santa María de Dota is the head town of Dota canton in San José Province, Costa Rica, up in the Talamanca mountains south of the capital. The Santa María district is at roughly 1,500 meters (about 5,000 feet) and had around 4,600 residents at the 2011 census. The canton as a whole is higher and larger, with just under 7,000 people in 2011.

## A bit of history

Dota was made a canton on July 23, 1925. It belongs to the Zona de los Santos ("Los Santos Zone"), a mountainous region that also includes the cantons of Tarrazú and León Cortés Castro.

## What it's known for

- **Coffee.** Los Santos is one of Costa Rica's best-known coffee-growing regions, and the high elevation and volcanic soil are a big part of why. The harvest runs roughly from November onward.
- **Cooperatives.** Local growers sell through farmer-owned co-ops such as Coopedota.
- **Cloud forest.** The surrounding mountains hold some of Costa Rica's highland forest and bird life, including the resplendent quetzal.

## Getting there

Roughly two hours by road from San José, climbing through coffee terraces. The air gets noticeably cooler as you go up.
